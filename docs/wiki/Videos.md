# Videos

Every OriginPass video in one place: the walkthrough of the Deliverable 3 features and the
five launch videos, narrated by Emmanuel. Select a picture or a title to open the file.

## Deliverable 3 walkthrough

| | Video | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| [![Walkthrough of the Deliverable 3 features](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/Pacha-e/OriginPass/images/videos/recorrido.jpg)](https://github.com/Pacha-e/OriginPass/releases/download/originpass-kit-portatil-2026-10-15/OriginPass-Entrega-3-recorrido.mp4) | [**OriginPass — Entrega 3: recorrido de las funciones nuevas**](https://github.com/Pacha-e/OriginPass/releases/download/originpass-kit-portatil-2026-10-15/OriginPass-Entrega-3-recorrido.mp4) | Horizontal 16:9, 1920×1080 | 3:13 |

Every new feature of Sprint 3, recorded from the running application on the
demonstration data, with a caption on screen that says what each step is. It runs in the
order of the live demonstration: the workshop issues a passport, the buyer checks it, the
product passes to a distributor and on to a buyer who claims it with two codes, the
analytics raise the copy alert, the passport is revoked, and the administrator reads the
signed audit log. It is recorded by
[`tools/video/record_features.py`](https://github.com/Pacha-e/OriginPass/blob/main/tools/video/record_features.py),
so it can be recorded again whenever the product changes.

The [presentation of the Sprint Review](https://github.com/Pacha-e/OriginPass/releases/download/originpass-kit-portatil-2026-10-15/OriginPass-Entrega-3-presentacion.pdf)
explains each feature slide by slide; both are in the
[kit release](https://github.com/Pacha-e/OriginPass/releases/tag/originpass-kit-portatil-2026-10-15).

The complete kit now includes all five launch videos, their credits and the feature
walkthrough. Extract the whole archive
and open `ABRIR-KIT.html` to select a local file. No network is needed to play these files.

**What the videos say and what the MVP does.** The launch videos speak about the product
as it is meant to become. In this MVP the passport is signed with a key held by the
server, not by the workshop, and a scan certifies the state of the record, not by itself
the physical piece; the scope is set out on [Deliverable 3](Deliverable-3).

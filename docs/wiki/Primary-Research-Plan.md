# Primary Research Plan

The desk research on the [Problem Validation](Problem-Validation) page settled assumption A3 and left A1, A2 and A4 open. Those three are questions about behaviour, and no public register answers them. This page is what it takes to close them, written so the work can be started the day it is read.

## What is still open

| Assumption | Question it really asks | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Do producers lose sales they can point to, because of imitations? | Producer interviews |
| A2 | Would a buyer check authenticity before paying? | Buyer survey |
| A4 | Would a check that needs an account or an app be abandoned? | Buyer survey |

## The buyer survey

Target: thirty or more people who have bought a craft or a branded product in the past year.

Build it in Google Forms. Every question below is ready to paste, with its type and its options. The wording avoids leading the answer, which is why none of the questions mentions OriginPass or authenticity technology.

**Title:** Comprar productos auténticos en Colombia
**Description:** Esta encuesta hace parte de un proyecto universitario en la Universidad EAFIT. Son diez preguntas y toma unos tres minutos. Las respuestas son anónimas.

| # | Question (Spanish, as respondents see it) | Type | Options | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | En el último año, ¿compraste alguna artesanía o producto de marca sin estar seguro de que fuera original? | Multiple choice | Sí / No / No estoy seguro | A2 |
| 2 | Si respondiste que sí, ¿qué hiciste al respecto? | Short answer | — | A2 |
| 3 | Antes de pagar, ¿qué tan importante es para ti saber que el producto es original? | Linear scale 1–5 | 1 = Nada importante, 5 = Muy importante | A2 |
| 4 | ¿Alguna vez decidiste no comprar algo porque dudabas de su autenticidad? | Multiple choice | Sí / No | A2 |
| 5 | ¿Cómo verificas hoy que un producto es original, si es que lo haces? | Checkboxes | Le pregunto al vendedor / Reviso la etiqueta o el empaque / Busco en internet / Compro solo en tiendas conocidas / No verifico / Otro | A3 |
| 6 | ¿Escanearías un código en la etiqueta para verificar el producto antes de pagar? | Multiple choice | Sí / No / Depende | A4 |
| 7 | ¿Instalarías una aplicación para hacer esa verificación? | Multiple choice | Sí / No | A4 |
| 8 | ¿Crearías una cuenta para hacer esa verificación? | Multiple choice | Sí / No | A4 |
| 9 | ¿Cuánto tiempo estarías dispuesto a dedicarle a esa verificación? | Multiple choice | Menos de 10 segundos / Entre 10 y 30 segundos / Más de 30 segundos / No lo haría | A4 |
| 10 | ¿Saber por dónde pasó el producto antes de llegar a la tienda cambiaría tu confianza en él? | Linear scale 1–5 | 1 = Nada, 5 = Mucho | Custody chain |

Questions 6, 7 and 8 are deliberately three separate questions rather than one. If A4 holds, the gap between the answers to 6 and to 7 and 8 is the finding, and a single combined question would hide it.

### Where to find thirty respondents

Anyone who has bought a craft or a branded item qualifies, so the sample does not need to be exotic. What it does need is to not be only engineering students, since that skews every question about technology.

- University groups outside the systems engineering programme
- Family and neighbourhood WhatsApp groups, which reach an older and less technical sample
- Tourist-facing craft shops in Medellín, asking the shopkeeper to pass on the link

Record where the responses came from. A sample drawn entirely from one channel is worth saying out loud in the conclusions.

## The producer interviews

Target: five to eight producers, mixing artisan workshops with small registered manufacturers. The interview guide is on the [Problem Validation](Problem-Validation) page and does not need rewriting.

### Channels worth trying, in order

**The Resguardo Indígena Zenú de San Andrés de Sotavento.** This is the single holder of an authorisation of use over the Tejeduría Zenú denomination — the one registration found in the desk research. The SIC publishes `indiozenu@yahoo.com` as its contact on the [Tejeduría Zenú page](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/temas/propiedad-industrial/direccion-de-signos-distintivos/denominaciones-de-origen/tejeduria-zenu). No other party in the country is better placed to say why that number is one and not two hundred.

**The Feria Artesanal y Cultural del Sombrero Fino Vueltiao, Tuchín.** Held annually, most recently in its twenty-seventh edition in January. Dozens of producers in one place over a weekend, which is the cheapest way to run five interviews. If the timing does not fit this semester, the organisers are still the fastest route to a list of workshops.

**Artesanías de Colombia.** Runs the Registro Único de Artesanos de Colombia. The public portal publishes no sector figures, so the register itself is the thing to ask for, through the institutional contact form.

**Alcaldía de Tuchín.** The municipality promotes the craft and can introduce a student project to workshops that have no web presence at all, which is most of them.

Approach institutions rather than compiling individual artisans' phone numbers from social media. It is more likely to work and it does not turn a university project into a scraping exercise.

### The message to send

Short, honest about being a student, clear that it costs them twenty minutes and asks for nothing.

> Asunto: Estudiante de EAFIT — 20 minutos sobre falsificación de artesanías
>
> Buenos días.
>
> Mi nombre es Emmanuel Hernández, soy estudiante de Ingeniería de Sistemas en la Universidad EAFIT. Estoy trabajando en un proyecto de la universidad sobre cómo un comprador puede saber que una artesanía es auténtica, y sobre qué pierde el productor cuando no puede demostrarlo.
>
> Antes de construir nada quiero entender el problema de boca de quien lo vive. Me gustaría hacerles unas preguntas, unos veinte minutos, por llamada o en persona si están cerca. No estoy vendiendo nada y no necesito ningún dato de su negocio: me interesa su experiencia.
>
> Una cosa que encontré revisando los registros públicos de la Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio: la Tejeduría Zenú está protegida con denominación de origen desde 2011, y en quince años solo hay una autorización de uso registrada. Me gustaría entender por qué.
>
> Quedo atento a si es posible.
>
> Emmanuel Hernández Melo
> ehernandem@eafit.edu.co

The last paragraph is there on purpose. It shows the work was done before asking for their time, and it asks a question they are uniquely able to answer.

## Recording the results

For each interview record the date, the role, the type of producer and the answers. Do not summarise away the parts that contradict the assumptions, since those are the useful ones. Results go into the Results table on the [Problem Validation](Problem-Validation) page, and the verdict on each assumption is written there whether it survives or not.

An assumption that fails and forces a change of scope is a better outcome than four that are confirmed. If buyers say they would not scan anything, that is worth knowing in August rather than in November.

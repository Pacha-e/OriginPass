## Working agreement

Because the roles sit with one person, the risk is that priority decisions and implementation decisions blur together. Two habits guard against it:

- A requirement is only accepted when its acceptance criteria are met and can be demonstrated. Criteria are written before the work starts, not after.
- Weekly progress is recorded in writing on the [Development Log](Development-Log) page, answering the same three questions a team would answer aloud: what was done, what comes next, and what is in the way.

## Capacity

Scope decisions on the [Requirements Prioritisation](Requirements-Prioritisation) page are made against that budget rather than against an ideal feature list.

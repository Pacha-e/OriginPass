# Product Vision Board

## Product vision statement

> Is a service for artisans, small manufacturers and the people who buy from them, who have to prove that a physical product is genuine and trace where it came from. It is called **OriginPass** and is a web application that issues a verifiable digital passport for every product and records each change of custody along its journey. Unlike printed certificates and brand-owned label checkers, our service lets any buyer verify a product without creating an account, and shows the full custody chain from maker to current owner.

## The board

### 1. Vision

Make the origin of any physical product provable, so that buying something genuine stops depending on trusting whoever is selling it.

### 2. Target group

| Group | Description |
|---|---|
| Artisan workshops | Producers of protected-origin crafts, often informal, without a trademark or the budget to defend one. They lose sales to imitations sold next to their own work. |
| Small and medium manufacturers | Registered companies that can prove they exist through the chamber of commerce, but cannot prove that a specific unit in a shop came from their production line. |
| Distributors and retailers | Intermediaries who need to show that the stock they hold was received from a legitimate source. |
| Buyers | People purchasing craft or branded goods, in person or online, who want to check authenticity before paying. They will not install an app or create an account to do it. |
| Platform administrator | Reviews and approves companies, and steps in when a product or a company has to be withdrawn. |

### 3. Needs

| Need | Who has it |
|---|---|
| Prove that a specific unit was made by a specific producer | Artisan workshops, manufacturers |
| Check authenticity at the moment of purchase, with no account and no app | Buyers |
| Distinguish a verified producer from anyone who claims to be one | Buyers, administrator |
| Show where a product has been between the maker and the shelf | Distributors, retailers, buyers |
| Withdraw a passport when a unit is stolen, faulty or fraudulent | Manufacturers, administrator |
| Notice that a passport is being copied before the damage spreads | Manufacturers, administrator |

### 4. Product

Standout features:

- **Two verification tracks.** Commercial companies verify through an official registry code. Artisan workshops, which usually have no such code, are approved through manual review. Neither track excludes the other kind of producer.
- **One passport per unit, not per product line.** Each individual item carries its own code, so a single leaked code cannot legitimise a whole batch of copies.
- **Public verification with no barrier.** The QR opens a page that works for anyone, without login, on any phone browser.
- **Custody chain.** Every handover from maker to distributor to buyer is recorded in order and cannot be edited afterwards.
- **Scan analytics.** Verification scans are analysed to reveal patterns that suggest a QR has been cloned, such as one passport appearing in distant places within a short window.
- **Reasoned withdrawal.** Rejecting a company or revoking a product always records why, so the decision can be reviewed later.

### 5. Business goals

- Become the origin registry that Colombian artisan sectors trust and adopt.
- Protect the commercial value of denominations of origin by making imitation detectable at the point of sale.
- Turn accumulated verification data into an early-warning signal for counterfeiting, valuable to producers and to the bodies that certify origin.
- Keep the cost of joining low enough that an informal workshop can register without paperwork it does not have.

## What makes it different

| Existing approach | Its limit | What OriginPass does instead |
|---|---|---|
| Printed certificate or hologram sticker | Can be photocopied or bought in bulk; nothing links it to one unit | Each unit carries a code that cannot be derived from another code |
| Brand-owned verification app | Only large brands can fund one; a buyer would need a different app per brand | One public page, no app, open to producers of any size |
| Trusting the seller or the marketplace | Fails exactly where counterfeiting happens, at the informal point of sale | Verification does not depend on the seller's word |
| Internal traceability software | Stops at the factory gate | Chain continues through distribution to the final owner |

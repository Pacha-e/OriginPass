# OriginPass Elevator Pitch

How can you be sure that what you are buying is actually authentic?

The truth is that, in most cases, there is no easy way to prove it. Everything comes down to trust: trusting what the seller says, the label, or the packaging.

The problem is that an imitation can look exactly like the original product. A buyer may end up paying for something fake, while legitimate companies and artisans lose sales and credibility.

That is why I created OriginPass, a platform that allows companies and artisans to generate a unique digital passport for every product.

By simply scanning a QR code, anyone can see who made the product, where it comes from, and the journey it has taken.

I am also aware that a QR code can be copied. That is why OriginPass records scans and transfers to detect possible suspicious activity.

I am currently looking for companies or artisans interested in testing OriginPass with real products.

Because buying something authentic should not depend only on trust.

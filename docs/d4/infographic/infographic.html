Project overview

OriginPass — a verifiable digital passport for every physical product.

Team: Emmanuel Hernández Melo, individual project.

Product Owner: Emmanuel Hernández Melo, who also acts as Scrum Master and developer.

Problem

Imitations of Colombian crafts and small brands are sold next to the genuine piece. Printed certificates can be photocopied, brand-owned apps serve only large brands, and a buyer at a market stall cannot check, before paying, who made a piece or how it reached the stall.

Proposed solution

OriginPass issues a digital passport for every physical unit, with a QR code that anyone can scan without an account or an app. The public page gives a verdict — genuine, revoked, altered or not found — names the verified producer and shows the chain of custody from the maker to the current owner. Companies are approved through an official registry and artisan workshops through a manual review. Every handover is signed and chained, every withdrawal states its reason, and scan analytics can warn a producer when one code appears in two regions at once.

Implemented features

Verify with no account

Scan the QR and read the verdict: genuine, revoked, altered or not found. FR28–FR31, FR62

One passport per unit

A code that cannot be derived from another, and a printable QR with the OriginPass mark. FR21, FR22, FR61

Chain of custody

Every handover is signed and chained; the buyer claims the piece with two codes, reading the QR by camera or from a photo. FR32, FR35–FR40, FR60

Verified producers

Official registry for companies, manual review for artisan workshops, and a public profile. FR07–FR18

Revocation and audit

A withdrawal always states its reason, and every status change goes to a signed audit log. FR41–FR45

Scan analytics

Scans by date and region, product ranking, trend, CSV export and a duplicate-scan alert. FR46–FR52

Accounts and abuse resistance

Registration, login lockout, password reset and change, and a cap on stored scans. FR01–FR06, FR57–FR59
